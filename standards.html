Clip standards

How Fio do Corte chooses, edits, credits and publishes clips.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

On this page

  3. Sensitive subjects

1. Sources

We clip only shows whose owners allow it, through a public clipping policy or direct permission. Credit alone is not permission, so a show without permission is not clipped.

  • For every clip, the studio records the show, the episode, the link and the start and end times.
  • Every post credits the show it came from.
  • If a show withdraws permission, we turn that source off in the studio and stop making clips from it.

2. Context and titles

A clip has to keep the meaning it had in the full conversation.

  • We cut where a thought starts and ends, not in the middle of a sentence.
  • Titles and on-screen text describe what was said. They never attribute to someone words they did not say.
  • A question, a hypothesis or a joke is never presented as a statement.
  • We avoid empty superlatives and promises the clip does not deliver.
  • Captions follow the speakers' own words, and the speakers' audio is never edited to change what they meant.

3. Sensitive subjects

The studio flags clips about elections and conspiracy theories and keeps them out of automatic publishing. They are posted only if the creator reviews them and decides to publish. Clips that break a platform's community guidelines are not posted at all.

4. Music, images and effects

  • Background music is royalty-free and stays below the voices. We do not use commercial songs.
  • Supporting images and stock video come from free-licence libraries such as Mixkit, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons. We use only public domain, CC0 or CC BY material, and CC BY material is credited in the post.
  • Sound effects come from royalty-free libraries.

5. How we use AI

The studio uses an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) to read the episode's transcript and suggest moments, titles, a cover headline and a quality score from 0 to 10. It also helps choose supporting images that match what is being said.

  • AI never writes words for the people in a clip. Captions come from what they said.
  • Clips with a weak score stay out of the schedule until they are fixed.
  • The AI receives the transcript and the clip text only. It never receives account data or access tokens.

6. Platform rules

Each clip follows the terms and community guidelines of the platform where it is posted.

  • Posts are published only through each platform's official API, on accounts that Fio do Corte owns.
  • On TikTok, every post is approved by the creator, who chooses who can view it, which interactions are allowed and whether it is commercial content.
  • We never buy followers, views or engagement, and the studio does not like, comment or follow on anyone's behalf.

7. Removal requests

If you own the rights to a show we clipped, or you appear in a clip and want it taken down, write to joseeduardopavezi@gmail.com with the link to the clip. We remove it from YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and if you ask, we stop clipping the show.

You can also use each platform's own copyright or privacy report form.
