About Fio do Corte

O trecho passa.
A ideia fica.

Fio do Corte is a Brazilian channel of clips from podcasts and long conversations. The name joins the thread of a conversation (fio) with the act of cutting out a moment (corte).

Our line says it in Portuguese: the clip passes, the idea stays. Every clip has to deliver an idea you can follow, a story worth hearing or a question worth continuing. Topics change from week to week; that rule does not.

Three kinds of clip

Clips are grouped into three series. They share one look, and the label tells you which kind of moment you are about to watch.

Conversas

Conversations. A question and the answer it gets, kept together so the answer still makes sense.

Ideias

Ideas. An explanation or a point of view that changes how you look at something.

Histórias

Stories. Experiences told by the people who lived them, from the first line to the turn.

Why there is a studio

A two-hour episode can hold one minute that deserves to travel on its own. Finding it, cutting it cleanly and crediting it properly takes time, so Fio do Corte built a studio for it.

Fio do Corte Studio is a desktop application that runs on our own computer. It prepares the clips, keeps the publishing schedule for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok, and asks for the creator's approval before anything is posted to TikTok. You can see each step on the How it works section and the rules we follow in our clip standards.

Who runs it

Fio do Corte and its studio are run by Jose Eduardo Pavezi, in Brazil. Questions, partnerships and removal requests go to the same address.
