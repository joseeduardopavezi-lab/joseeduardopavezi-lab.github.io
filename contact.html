What to write about

Removal requests

For rights holders and for people who appear in a clip. Send the link to the clip and tell us what you want removed. We take it down from every platform where it was posted.

Privacy and data

Ask what data the studio holds or ask us to delete it. The Privacy Policy lists everything the studio collects and why.

Shows and partnerships

If you host a show and want it clipped with credit, or want to talk about Fio do Corte, tell us about your show and where it is published.

To help us answer quickly

  • Include the link to the clip or post you are writing about.
  • Say who you are: the show, the guest or the account holder.
  • For a privacy request, say which account or data the request is about.
