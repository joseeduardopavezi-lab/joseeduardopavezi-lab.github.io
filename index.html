Fio do Corte
Studio

The studio behind Fio do Corte, a Brazilian channel of clips from podcasts and long conversations. It finds the strongest minute of an episode, edits it for vertical video and publishes it to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

See how it works Watch on TikTok

From a two-hour episode to a one-minute clip

The studio runs on the creator's computer and takes care of the slow parts of clipping: finding the moment, cutting it, captioning it and keeping the schedule. The creator decides what goes out.

  1. Pick the episode

    Episodes come only from shows that allow clips. The studio records the show, the episode and the timestamps, so every clip carries its credit.

  2. Find the moment

    Working from the episode's transcript, AI suggests moments that hold one complete idea. Each clip gets a quality score from 0 to 10.

  3. Edit for vertical

    The clip is cut to 9:16 with word-by-word captions, the show's credit and royalty-free music kept under the voices.

  4. Review and approve

    Clips wait in the studio's schedule, where the creator can watch, edit or remove any of them. A TikTok post goes out only after the creator approves it.

  5. Publish on time

    At the scheduled time the studio posts to the creator's own accounts, then reads the view counts to learn which clips work.

Nothing reaches TikTok without the creator's yes

Each TikTok post waits in an approval queue. The approval panel follows TikTok's Content Sharing Guidelines, and the studio sends the video only after the creator makes every choice below.

Destination account
The panel names the TikTok account that will receive the post, with its username and the scheduled time.
Who can view the video
Chosen from the options TikTok allows for the account. Nothing is preselected.
Comments, Duet and Stitch
All off until the creator turns them on. An interaction that is disabled on the account stays disabled.
Commercial content
The creator says whether the video promotes their own business or is a paid partnership, and the matching TikTok policy is shown.
Caption
Suggested by the studio and fully editable before approval.
Consent
The creator agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and authorizes the upload at the scheduled time. Any post can be cancelled before it goes out.

Where the clips go

The studio publishes only to accounts the creator owns and has connected through each platform's official sign-in. Each platform has its own rules, so each one works a little differently.

Clip standards

A clip is worth posting only if it keeps the meaning of the conversation and gives the show its credit.

Read the full standards
  • Authorized shows onlyWe clip shows that allow clipping, and every post names the show and the episode.
  • No words put in anyone's mouthTitles describe what was said. A question, a joke or a hypothesis is never turned into a statement.
  • Sensitive subjects stay off the scheduleClips about elections or conspiracy theories are never published automatically.
  • Removal on requestRights holders and people who appear in a clip can ask us to take it down.

The creator's data stays on the creator's computer

The studio connects only to the creator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Access tokens are stored encrypted with Windows Data Protection on that computer only. Account statistics are used to show performance inside the studio and nowhere else. Nothing is sold or shared, and the creator can disconnect any account at any time.
